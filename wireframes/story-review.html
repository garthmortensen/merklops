Lineage trace: why it changed

The only thing that changed was the drift window in steel_estimation. A shorter window picked up the faster recent rise in steel prices, so steel's estimated drift went from 2.9% to 3.6% a year. That raised the steel forecast, and because steel is one of nine equal-weight inputs, cost_index in 2030 rose by 1.2%. Nothing else moved: no other model reads steel prices.
